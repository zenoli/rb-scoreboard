## Draft Strategy Cheat Sheet

| Tip | Explanation |
|-----|-------------|
| **Cards = free points** | YC/RC are +1 each. Physical players (Gyokeres 5YC, Sorloth 4YC+1RC, Dumfries, Montiel) are more valuable than in standard fantasy |
| **Max 1 per country** | Can't stack Argentina or France. Best combos: FRA forward + NOR mid, or ENG forward + FRA mid |
| **Penalty takers** | Haaland (NOR), Oyarzabal (ESP), Calhanoglu (TUR), Mbappe (FRA) — extra goal upside |
| **New country sleepers** | Norway, Sweden, Turkiye, Austria, Cote d'Ivoire have elite talent with no WC history to compare |
| **Avoid** | Neymar (injured), Perisic (37), Enner Valencia (36), Isak (injury-disrupted season) |
| **Coach pick** | France or Argentina for the 10pt winner bonus |
